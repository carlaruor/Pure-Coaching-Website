Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Pure Coaching intends to collect, use, store and protect personal information shared through our website and services.

Draft last updated: 19 November 2026

Draft for business approval

This policy must be reviewed and approved by Pure Coaching before publication and before the online intake form is activated. The retention schedule, staff document-access process and uploaded-file handling procedure are not yet finalised.

Introduction and our commitment to privacy

Pure Coaching respects the privacy of participants, families, carers, referrers and other people who contact us. We aim to handle personal information carefully, transparently and in accordance with applicable Australian privacy requirements.

This policy explains the information we may collect, why we collect it and how people can contact us about their information. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we use safeguards designed to reduce privacy and security risks.

Approval required: Pure Coaching should obtain appropriate advice to confirm which privacy laws, NDIS requirements and record-keeping obligations apply to its operations.

Information we collect

Depending on the enquiry, referral or support requested, we may collect:

  • contact details, including names, email addresses and phone numbers;
  • details about the participant and the person making a referral on their behalf;
  • information about support needs, goals, preferred contact arrangements and service interests;
  • NDIS information, including plan and funding-management details;
  • disability-related or other sensitive information voluntarily provided to help us understand support needs; and

Please provide only information that is relevant to the enquiry. A referrer should ensure they are authorised to share another person's information.

How we use information

We may use submitted information to respond to enquiries, consider referrals, understand requested supports, discuss service arrangements, communicate about next steps and maintain appropriate business records.

We do not use uploaded NDIS documents as email attachments. Staff notifications may include submitted form details and indicate that private documents have been stored.

Secure storage and access

The online intake system is designed to store validated submissions in Supabase. Uploaded documents are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket, and the website does not provide public document links.

Access should be limited to personnel authorised by Pure Coaching who need the information for legitimate work purposes. Technical measures include private storage controls, server-side validation, anti-spam checks and restricted submission origins.

Approval required: Before accepting live submissions, Pure Coaching must approve staff access roles, access review and logging, incident response, secure download practices and malware-scanning or quarantine arrangements for uploaded files.

Information sharing

Information may be shared internally with relevant Pure Coaching personnel where needed to respond to an enquiry, assess a referral or arrange support.

Service providers may process limited information on our behalf. The planned online form uses Supabase for secure submission and private document storage, Resend for staff notification emails and Cloudflare Turnstile for spam protection. Information may also be disclosed where authorised by the person concerned or required by law.

Approval required: Pure Coaching should review its agreements with service providers, their processing locations and any required overseas-disclosure wording before this policy is published.

Information retention and secure deletion

Personal information should be kept only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected and any applicable legal, funding or record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer required, Pure Coaching should take reasonable steps to securely delete or de-identify it.

Approval required: No specific retention period is stated in this draft. Pure Coaching must approve a retention schedule and implement an audited process that removes both submission records and related private documents before the online form is activated.

Access, correction and privacy concerns

You may contact Pure Coaching to request access to personal information held about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and authority before providing access or making a change.

We will consider requests and concerns in accordance with applicable requirements. Some information may need to be retained or access may be limited where permitted or required by law.

Contact details and policy updates

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or concerns about how information has been handled, contact Pure Coaching:

Email: info@pure-coaching.com.au

Phone: 0407 234 842

We may update this policy when our practices, systems or legal obligations change. The last-updated date at the top of the page will identify the current draft or published version.
