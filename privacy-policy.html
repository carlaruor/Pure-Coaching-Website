Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Pure Coaching collects, uses, stores and protects personal information shared through our website and services.

Draft last updated: 24 November 2026

Introduction and our commitment to privacy

Pure Coaching respects the privacy of participants, families, carers, referrers and other people who contact us. We handle personal information carefully and openly. We follow the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

This policy explains the information we may collect, why we collect it and how people can contact us about their information. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we use safeguards to protect personal information.

Information we collect

Depending on the enquiry, referral or support requested, we may collect:

  • contact details, including names, email addresses and phone numbers;
  • details about the participant and the person making a referral on their behalf;
  • information about support needs, goals, preferred contact arrangements and service interests;
  • NDIS information, including plan and funding-management details;
  • disability-related or other sensitive information voluntarily provided to help us understand support needs;
  • documents you give us, such as NDIS plans, reports and assessments.

Please provide only information that is relevant to the enquiry. A referrer must be authorised to share another person's information, or must confirm that the person knows about the referral.

How we collect information

We collect personal information directly from you. We may also collect it from a referrer, family member, carer, Local Area Coordinator or the NDIA, when you have agreed or when the law allows.

Our website

When you use our website, our spam check may collect technical information such as your IP address.

How we use information

We may use submitted information to respond to enquiries, consider referrals, understand requested supports, discuss service arrangements, communicate about next steps and maintain appropriate business records.

We do not send uploaded documents by email. Staff notification emails only tell us a new submission has arrived. They do not contain form details or documents.

Marketing

We do not use your personal information for marketing.

Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making to make decisions about people.

Secure storage and access

The online intake system stores validated submissions in Supabase, hosted in Sydney, Australia. Uploaded documents are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket, and the website does not provide public document links.

Access is limited to staff authorised by Pure Coaching who need the information for work purposes. Technical measures include private storage, anti-spam checks and limits on where submissions can come from.

Data Breaches

If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Information sharing

Information may be shared internally with relevant Pure Coaching personnel where needed to respond to an enquiry, assess a referral or arrange support.

Service providers may process limited information on our behalf. Our online form uses Supabase for secure submission and private document storage, Resend for staff notification emails and Cloudflare Turnstile for spam protection. Information may also be disclosed where authorised by the person concerned or required by law.

Overseas Storage and Processing of Personal Information

Some of our service providers store or process personal information outside Australia.

  • Supabase: Australia (Sydney), with certain processing activities potentially taking place in Singapore, the United States, Europe and other locations.
  • Resend: United States.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Global infrastructure, including the United States and the European Economic Area.

When we may share information

We may share information with: the NDIA; the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission; approved auditors; plan managers; other providers involved in your supports; your guardian, nominee or authorised representative; and anyone who needs it to prevent a serious safety risk. We only share what is needed.

Information retention and secure deletion

If an enquiry does not go ahead, we delete the information after 12 months.

If you become a participant, we keep your records for 7 years after your supports end, or until you turn 25 if you are under 18. We then securely delete them.

Access, correction and privacy concerns

You may contact Pure Coaching to request access to personal information held about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and authority before providing access or making a change.

We respond to access and correction requests within 30 days. Some information may need to be retained or access may be limited where permitted or required by law.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, contact us. We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days and respond in writing. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission (ndiscommission.gov.au).

Support to Make Decisions

If you need support to make decisions, you can choose someone to help you. We deal with your guardian, nominee or authorised representative where they have the legal authority to act for you.

Accessible Formats

This policy is available in Easy Read and other formats on request. You can contact us by phone, email or in person, through the National Relay Service, or with an interpreter.

Contact details and policy updates
