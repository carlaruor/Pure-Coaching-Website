About Pure Coaching

Support that feels personal, active and real.

Pure Coaching is a Cairns-based support team helping people build confidence, independence and everyday skills through genuine relationships and real-life experiences.

We work alongside the people we support, creating opportunities to learn, connect, try new things and move towards the goals that matter to them.

How we work

We believe good support means getting involved.

Our role isn't simply to be there. We participate, encourage and work alongside each person — whether that's building everyday skills, getting active, connecting with the community or working towards a personal goal.

  1. 01

    Personal

    Built around the individual, not a fixed program.

  2. 02

    Involved

    We take part, build relationships and experience things together.

  3. 03

    Progress-focused

    We focus on meaningful progress, from everyday wins to bigger goals.

Meet Pure Coaching

The people behind the support.

Our team brings different personalities, interests and experiences, but we share the same approach — showing up, getting involved and helping people move towards what matters to them.
