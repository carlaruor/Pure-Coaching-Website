About Pure Coaching

Support that feels personal, active and real.

Pure Coaching is a Cairns-based support team helping people build confidence, independence and everyday skills through genuine relationships and real-life experiences.

We work alongside the people we support, creating opportunities to learn, connect, try new things and move towards the goals that matter to them.

How we work

We believe good support means getting involved.

We participate, encourage and work alongside each person — building skills, confidence and independence through real-life experiences.

  1. 01

    Personal

    Built around the individual, not a fixed program.

  2. 02

    Involved

    We take part, build relationships and experience things together.

  3. 03

    Progress-focused

    Meaningful progress, from everyday wins to bigger goals.
