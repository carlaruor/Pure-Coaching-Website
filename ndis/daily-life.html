Everyday skills. More independence.

Practical support

Help with the things that keep life moving.

Daily Life support is about doing everyday activities together—not taking over. Support can be adapted around your current skills, home, routines and goals, with encouragement to take the next step when you feel ready.

At home

  • Cooking and meal preparation
  • Cleaning and home organisation
  • Laundry and household skills

02

Planning and routines

  • Building manageable routines
  • Planning the week
  • Organising appointments and errands

03

Everyday decisions

  • Grocery shopping
  • Budgeting and everyday spending
  • Making choices with confidence

What can Daily Life look like with Pure Coaching?

Progress with purpose

Small steps can make everyday life feel more manageable.

The goal is not only to complete today's task. It is to create useful opportunities to practise, make choices and build capacity over time— in a way that is realistic for you.
