Support Coordination

Understand your plan.Know what comes next.

Support Coordination helps you understand your NDIS plan, connect with the right services and supports, and make informed decisions about how to use your funding.

How we can help

Making your plan easier to navigate.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Make sense of your plan.

    Understand your funding, what your plan includes and the supports available to you.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Find the right supports.

    Explore providers and services that match your needs, preferences and goals.

  3. 03

    Coordinate

    Bring your supports together.

    Help organise services, communicate with providers and keep your supports working together.

  4. 04

    Adapt

    Make changes when things aren't working.

    Work through challenges, explore different options and adjust your supports as your needs or circumstances change.

Your plan. Your choices.

You're in control.

Your Support Coordinator works alongside you to explore your options, solve problems and make informed choices about your supports — while keeping your goals and preferences at the centre.
