Employment Support

Find your direction.Take the next step.

Employment Support helps you explore what work could look like for you, build the skills and confidence to get started, and take practical steps towards finding and maintaining meaningful employment.

Starting with you

What could work look like for you?

Before job applications come direction. We can help you explore your strengths, interests and the environments you work best in, so you can discover realistic opportunities and decide what your next step could be.

  • 02What do you enjoy?

Real experiences

Employment support in action.

Practical support

Steps we can help you with.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Understanding interests, strengths and realistic employment options.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Building job-readiness skills and creating or improving a resume.

  3. 03

    Search

    Finding suitable jobs and learning how to search for opportunities.

  4. 04

    Apply

    Completing applications and responding to opportunities.

  5. 05

    Interview

    Preparing for interviews, practising responses and building confidence.

  6. 06

    Start

    Understanding workplace expectations and preparing to begin work.

  7. 07

    Maintain & Grow

    Building routines, confidence and independence to help maintain employment where appropriate.

Building independence

More than getting the job.

  • Confidence

    Communicating, asking questions and becoming more comfortable in a workplace.

  • Expectations

    Understanding routines, responsibilities, presentation and workplace behaviour.

  • Independence

    Getting organised, managing time and becoming less reliant on support.

  • Consistency

    Building routines that make employment more sustainable.

Personalised, goal-focused support helping people build confidence, independence and meaningful everyday lives across Cairns & FNQ.
